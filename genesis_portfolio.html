  • Inspired

    Inspired is a 2D puzzle platformer that depicts an artist (represented by a stick figure avatar) suffering from burnout seeking inspiration from browsing pictures on an art-sharing site infested by AI-generated content. Throughout their journey, the player character must utilise their tools as an artist to explore different artworks across the website and come face-to-face against AI imagery that physically manifests as corrupted creatures that attempt to ‘kill’ the player character’s motivation. At the end of a level, they will encounter a boss creature that is a cheap, artificially created imitation of the original artwork, which they unveil upon defeating the boss and uncover a certain element about the drawing that gives them inspiration for theirs.

    Gameplay involves the usage of commonly used tools in digital painting such as the Brush, Eraser, and Lasso tools. Atypical to common platforming tropes, the player character’s mobility is somewhat restrained, curtailed by a short jump distance which prevents them from effortlessly transitioning between pictures. Instead, players are encouraged to be creative with the utilisation of their tools to allow them to traverse their environment in a quick and effective manner. For example, the player can use the Lasso to latch onto a protruding object from a drawing positioned higher, therefore allowing them to climb towards that platform.

    Attempting to impede the player character is the antagonistic force of the game, an artificial intelligence (AI) image generating software entitled UDream, which manifests itself in the form of glitchy, corrupted beings. Some of these beings are level assets such as glitched blocks and spikes which may obstruct the level’s main pathway and must be overcome using alternative solutions, whereas some are mobile enemies who must either be evaded or defeated using the game’s basic combat system. This involves the usage of the brush to slash at weak spots visible on the enemies’ sprite and using the eraser as a makeshift shield to block incoming attacks.

  • Retro Revolt

    Retro Revolt is a 2D Metroidvania themed around the concept of lost media. It stars Cee Arty, a humanoid figure capable of manifesting physical projections from their giant, clunky CRT screen-shaped head. As a mysterious electrical storm engulfs the skies of Retropolis and corrupts its inhabitants to the will of an unseen evil, players must control Cee Arty through the various sections of Retropolis’ underground layout to defeat a gauntlet of mini-bosses, freeing their “souls” from the spirit’s corruption to gain their abilities or radio frequencies.

    Cee Arty's gameplay is defined by their unique Reality Byte ability, which enhances their abilities while in a more glitched, corrupted state; players must press the LB button to trigger Reality Byte, allowing Cee to perform Reality Byte abilities by performing specific inputs. For example, in the mid-game players can bind X and right to launch an uppercut fist from Cee’s screen, or bind X and up to launch a peashooter reminiscent of the weapons from the Cuphead series. Additional Reality Byte abilities can be unlocked by exploring the world of Retropolis, either as rewards from bosses that were purified, or as secret unlockables discovered by solving environmental puzzles across both the surface and the underground networks.
